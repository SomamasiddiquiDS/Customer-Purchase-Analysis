\# Business Insights – Customer Purchase Analysis



\## 1. Highest Revenue City

\- Lucknow generated the highest total purchase revenue among all cities.

\- This indicates strong customer purchasing activity in Lucknow.



\## 2. Highest Revenue Payment Method

\- Net Banking generated the highest total purchase revenue among all payment methods.

\- This indicates that Net Banking is an important payment channel in this dataset.



\## 3. Highest Revenue Customer Segment

\- VIP customers contributed the highest total purchase revenue.

\- This highlights the importance of high-value customers to overall sales.
